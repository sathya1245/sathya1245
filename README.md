## 🚀 Featured projects

### 🔮 [PriceProphet](https://github.com/sathya1245/Price-Prophet)
An AI-powered dynamic pricing platform that finds the right selling price using competitor intelligence, market trends, location-aware demand signals, and forecasting models — so businesses stop leaving money on the table.
`TypeScript`

### 🛣️ [STAR — Stochastic Traffic-Adaptive Replanning](https://github.com/sathya1245/star-path-planner)
Path planning and collision avoidance for autonomous vehicles on unstructured roads: a Traffic Entropy Index measures how chaotic traffic is, heavy-tailed risk fields predict erratic swerves, and a pre-computed trajectory portfolio swaps in escape manoeuvres in under 20 ms.
`MATLAB`

### 📖 [LexiRead](https://github.com/sathya1245/lexiread-voice)
An assistive learning platform for students with dyslexia. Bring a PDF, a photo of a page, or pasted text — the app then *listens to the student read*, following their voice at their pace, with live WPM tracking, practice-word flags, and a shared reading room. Runs entirely on browser-native speech APIs, so no audio ever leaves the device.
`JavaScript` `CSS`
